My name is Ahmed Akl. I'm a PhD researcher in Computer Science at Griffith University and CSIRO's Data61, based in Brisbane, Australia. I have over 10 years of academic and industry experience across machine learning, data science and software engineering.
My research focuses on multimodal AI: Vision-Language Models, Multimodal Large Language Models and Visual Question Answering. In particular, I work on making these models more robust and trustworthy by reducing object hallucination and helping them cope with distribution shift. Before my PhD, I worked as a Senior Machine Learning Engineer, an AI/ML Engineer and a Python Software Engineer, building NLP, computer vision and conversational AI systems, and I've published research in PLoS ONE and IEEE Access. I also love teaching, and lecture and tutor in data analytics, programming and research methods. I'm currently exploring Agentic AI and LLM-powered applications. I speak Arabic natively and English at a full professional level.
